Release Management for a 12-App PLM Platform

Organization
BJIT Limited — Client: Ericsson (Sweden), CPD team
Role
Release & DevOps Consultant (one of 5 release managers) (designation: Project Manager → Senior Project Manager (Jul 2023))
When
Aug 2021 – Feb 2025
Team
One of 5 peer release managers; supported 2–3 of 6 teams at a time
18 / 18monthly PROD releases on time
~100SIT and UAT deploys
50%faster deploys
16PROD instances on 4 servers

Context

Ericsson runs a distributed Dassault 3DEXPERIENCE platform: 12 apps, including 3DSpace, 3DPassport, 3DDashboard and full-text search, on separate servers. PROD ran on 4 Linux servers with 16 instances.

The problem

6 development teams shipped into the same platform. Every release had to land on time without breaking production.

What I did

  • Planned and owned release schedules: 3 SIT and 1–2 UAT deploys a month, and a monthly PROD release. 2–3 deploys a day to the test environments.
  • Found the code changes for each release and wrote the deployment plan from them.
  • Automated packaging and deployment with Jenkins, Ansible and Ant.
  • Designed the Gerrit branching strategy; led code review, merges and release-package QA.
  • Briefed the client before and after every release.
  • Maintained 3DSpace full-text search: index updates and data indexing.
  • Kept 6 teams on one delivery line without delaying any release; supported on-demand deploys.

Results

  • All 18 monthly PROD releases on time over 1.5 years.
  • ~100 SIT/UAT deploys.
  • Deploy time cut by 50%.

Role

When

Features

  • Offline dictionary lookup from .slob files (XDXF/HTML).
  • Bookmark and revisit words.
  • Word lists: GRE, IELTS and more.
  • Courses with a learning schedule.
  • Text-to-speech pronunciation.

How it's built

Flutter, Android first, with a multi-platform setup. Dictionary files are not bundled; free ones come from the Kiwix library and aard2.
